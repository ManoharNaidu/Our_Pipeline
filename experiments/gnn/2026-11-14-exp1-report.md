# Experiment 1 report: PMP neighbour selection vs Ours K=1 (Instagram)

Date: 2026-11-14. Decision IDs refer to [`2026-11-14-exp1-decisions.md`](2026-11-14-exp1-decisions.md). The longer
technical write-up (GNN grid, diagnostics, per-seed validation numbers, runtime) is
[`2026-11-14-exp1-gnn-selection-report.md`](2026-11-14-exp1-gnn-selection-report.md).

## Result

**Choosing neighbours by cosine similarity on PMP-L1 embeddings does not improve Ours on Instagram.** AUROC, the
primary metric, is unchanged within noise (−0.29, 95% CI −1.49 to +0.90, Holm p 1.0). Macro-F1 is lower on all 5
seeds (−1.96, CI −2.85 to −1.04, raw p 0.011) but not significant after Holm (p 0.119 over the 12 tests of E6; it was
0.065 over 6 tests (E5) and 0.033 over 3 tests (E3)). AUPRC is not significantly different (−0.53,
Holm p 1.0). The BWGNN arm is in [`2026-11-14-exp1-bwgnn-report.md`](2026-11-14-exp1-bwgnn-report.md); Reddit and the final
12-test family are in [`2026-11-15-exp1-reddit-report.md`](2026-11-15-exp1-reddit-report.md).

## Setup

1. **What changed:** for each target, the M = 4 neighbours on each metapath (K = 1) are ranked by cosine similarity of
   PMP 1-layer GNN embeddings instead of by diffusion (H3); the experiment config differs from Ours K=1 only in that (G4).
2. **What stayed the same:** Gemma-2-9B-it writes the summaries and is the LoRA classifier; prompts, `auroc_es3` epoch
   rule, M, K and all hyperparameters are those of Ours K=1. Unchanged node summaries were reused (H4).
3. **GNN:** PMP reimplemented from the official code (D1–D11), config Y3 chosen on validation AUROC (62.86); trained
   on train labels only, early-stopped on validation labels, test labels never read (tested).
4. **Seeds and split:** classifier seeds 0–4, paired with Ours K=1; split seed 0 (794 train, 794 validation,
   6,358 test nodes); one GNN (seed 0) for all five classifier seeds.
5. **Hardware:** one NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB; about 1.9 GPU-hours for this arm, peak 19.35 GB GPU memory per seed.

## Limitations

- One GNN seed (0) is shared by all five classifier seeds, so the spread above is classifier variance only (D10).
- The GNN is trained on a small label set: 72 fraud and 722 benign training nodes on Instagram.
- Reddit was skipped by decision (S5): about 9.4 GPU-hours, only 12.1% of Reddit targets have more than 4 candidate
  neighbours, and Reddit PMP-L1's best GNN epoch is 0. Its embeddings, diagnostics and config are kept.
- Amazon Instruments and BWGNN were not run (S1, S2).
- Only 51.8% of Instagram targets have more than 4 candidates; for the rest the selection cannot change the prompt.
- The original DGP row comes from another codebase with a different model (Qwen3-8B) and K = 2, and has no
  significance test, so its differences are context only.
